# Loch Kelly's Approach to IFS

Loch Kelly is an IFS-trained psychotherapist, meditation teacher, and author who
has developed an original synthesis of **Internal Family Systems (IFS)** parts
work with **Effortless Mindfulness** — a nondual meditation approach rooted in
contemplative traditions he studied beginning in the 1980s. He co-teaches
workshops with IFS founder Richard "Dick" Schwartz, and his method is often
labeled **EM+IFS** or **Self-Led IFS**.

## Core Thesis

Traditional IFS accesses Self by working *through* parts (asking parts to step
back / unblend). Kelly argues that Self is **ever-present and inherent** — not
something to be developed or constructed — and that Effortless Mindfulness
provides tools to **shift directly into Self** without having to negotiate with
every protective part first.

Key claim: "Only a limited level of psychological healing and growth can happen
without realizing Self." Parts work and awakening practice form a **positive
feedback loop** — mindfulness reveals Self, Self heals parts, healed parts make
awakening more stable.

## Distinguishing Features vs. Traditional IFS

| Traditional IFS | Loch Kelly's EM+IFS |
| --- | --- |
| Access Self by asking parts to unblend | Shift local awareness directly into open/awake awareness |
| Self emerges as parts step back | Self is uncovered as already-here |
| Questions asked *of* parts | Questions asked *of Self*: "Who is aware?" "Who is the one asking?" |
| 8 C's as qualities of Self | 8 C's used as **entry points** into Self; adds "11 i's" of Self Essence |
| 6 F's to work with parts | A modified 6 F's used to **identify Self** and distinguish it from "Self-like parts" |

## Signature Techniques

- **Unhooking local awareness** — unblending from thinking or a part by letting
  awareness "turn around" to spacious awareness or drop down into the body.
- **Glimpse practices** — short shifts of the center of perception from
  ego-location to "seeing from Being." A glimpse is explicitly *not* a state,
  altered consciousness, or belief; it's a relocation of the perceiver. Packaged
  in his **Mindful Glimpses** app.
- **"What is here when there is no problem to solve?"** — a guided inquiry /
  meditation he uses to drop practitioners into Self directly.
- **Embodied parts engagement** — locate a part in the body (shape, size, color,
  tone), thank it, request space, then feel Self-energy arise in the opened
  space.
- **Spotting Self-like parts** — explicit training to distinguish authentic Self
  from four common imitators (calm manager parts, spiritual-bypass parts, etc.).

## EM+IFS Training Structure

**Level 1 — Living from Awakened Self Leadership**
- Self-Led IFS in daily life when blended
- 11 i's of Self Essence
- Kelly's variant of the 6 F's for identifying Self
- Four Self-like parts to watch for
- Avoiding spiritual bypass / "psychological underpass" / "intellectual overpass"

**Level 2 — Fine-Tuning Access**
- How EM unlocks IFS effectiveness
- Subtle cues between Self and Self-like parts
- Spotting ego-manager blending during ordinary activity
- Conscious relationship with the ego-manager part

**Level 3 — The Dance of Self and Parts**
- Structure of the parts-based psyche
- 8 C's as entry points
- Distinguishing protectors and exiles from authentic Self
- Effortless Mindfulness vs. conventional mindfulness

## Stance on Trauma

Kelly frames trauma integration as a **"detox or thawing-out process"** rather
than a dark night / spiritual emergency, because stabilized Self-awareness
provides the resource to "bear what seemed unbearable." Awakening and healing
are treated as complementary, not sequential.
