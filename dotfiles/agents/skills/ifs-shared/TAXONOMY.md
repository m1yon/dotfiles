# TAXONOMY.md — manager / firefighter / exile

Standard IFS three-fold parts taxonomy, retained by Loch Kelly's EM+IFS. Lives in part-page frontmatter as `part_type`.

## Manager

**Role:** proactive protection. Manages day-to-day life to keep exiles from being activated. Plans, anticipates, perfects, rehearses, controls.

**Texture:** future-oriented anxiety, double-checking, productivity-as-safety, perfectionism, people-pleasing, intellectualizing, caretaker, inner critic.

**What gives it away:** *"if I just keep [X] under control, [Y] won't happen."* The X is observable behavior; the Y is the exile's fear. Managers are organized around prevention.

**Common phrasings:**
- *"won't let me stop once I start"*
- *"catches every mistake before it happens"*
- *"plans the conversation in advance"*
- *"reads the room"*

## Firefighter

**Role:** reactive protection. Activates when an exile breaks through despite the managers' efforts. Job is to put out the fire fast — distract, numb, dissociate, override.

**Texture:** impulsive, present-moment, "I need this NOW", binge, rage, dissociation, substance use, compulsive scrolling, sex, shopping, self-harm, suicidal ideation as escape (distinct from active SI).

**What gives it away:** the action is disproportionate to the trigger and feels urgent. The relief is real but short. Firefighters are organized around shutting down feeling, not preventing it.

**Common phrasings:**
- *"the part that just wants to numb out"*
- *"flips the table when X"*
- *"the rage that comes out of nowhere"*
- *"the one that says 'fuck it, I'm done'"*

## Exile

**Role:** holds the original burden. Young, vulnerable, carrying extreme feelings or beliefs from formative experiences. Managers and firefighters exist to keep the exile contained.

**Texture:** young, small, frozen-in-time, helpless, ashamed, terrified, abandoned, unworthy. Bodily often felt as smallness, throat constriction, chest collapse, "the kid in there."

**What gives it away:** age-felt is young (often single digits). Naming it usually surfaces a body sensation that pre-dates words.

**Common phrasings:**
- *"the small one"*
- *"the kid at the window"*
- *"the one that froze"*
- *"the part that doesn't believe anyone's coming"*

## Reclassification is normal

Type lives in frontmatter, not folder structure, because reclassification happens. A part first met as a manager may turn out to be a firefighter once its trigger pattern is clearer. Update `part_type:` when the user's understanding shifts; do not split into a new part page.

## Unknown

Default `part_type: unknown` when type isn't yet clear. No need to force a classification at first contact.
