# Crisis Plan

Author this in a clear, non-crisis moment. Rewrite the body below in your own words. The `/em-ifs-session` skill never edits this page; what you write here is what you read when it links you back.

What lives here: 2–3 concrete pre-committed actions you'll actually do. Specific, not abstract. Things you can do alone if needed. Not a hotline, not a therapist — a regulation protocol you trust because you wrote it.

## If I'm here, I will

- Text [name of person] — say "checking in, having a hard moment."
- 20-min walk around the block. No phone in hand.

## Replace the items above with your own

Examples of what concrete looks like (do not copy verbatim — write yours):

- "Cold water on face for 30s, then drink a glass of water slowly."
- "Sit on the porch with the dog for 10 minutes before deciding anything."
- "Call [therapist's after-hours line]: [number]."
- "Don't make any decisions until I've slept."

When you're done editing, your plan should fit on one screen and feel like instructions you can follow without thinking.
